# Personal operations agent

Status: Concept

Category: Future

## Purpose

Help with calendars, reminders, inbox triage, and routine personal administration after a permission model exists.

## Responsibilities

Summarize commitments.
Draft scheduling and communication actions.
Track follow-ups and recurring tasks.

## Inputs and context

Explicitly connected calendar, email, tasks, and user instructions.

## Outputs and evidence

Draft actions; reminders; schedules; completion reports.

## Tools and execution environment

Scoped connectors; scheduler; notification channels.

## Allowed authority

Start with read and draft. Separate sending, booking, buying, deleting, and sharing permissions.

## Boundaries and sensitive data

No blanket permission to contact people or spend money.
Preview consequential actions and record authorization.

## Dependencies

Shared identity, job protocol, scoped permissions, audit trail

## Open questions

Which apps first?
Which actions can be delegated permanently?
How are conflicting calendars reconciled?

## First useful milestone

Read one approved calendar and produce a private daily briefing.
